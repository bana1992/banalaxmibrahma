About

I am an Assistant Professor (Grade-II) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar.

I completed my Ph.D. from the National Institute of Technology Bhopal. My research involves the application of artificial intelligence and machine learning to problems involving renewable energy and photovoltaic systems.

My work combines data-driven modelling with physical knowledge of the underlying systems, with an emphasis on forecasting, modelling and uncertainty estimation.

Research

My current research interests include:

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning and Time-Series Forecasting
  • Solar Irradiance and Renewable Energy Forecasting
  • Photovoltaic Power Modelling and Forecasting
  • Physics-Informed Machine Learning
  • Uncertainty Quantification and Prediction Intervals
  • Spatiotemporal Modelling of Renewable Energy Data
