About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar.

I completed my Ph.D. from the National Institute of Technology Bhopal. My academic work combines machine learning, deep learning and data-driven modelling with applications in renewable energy and photovoltaic systems.

Research Interests

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning for Time-Series Forecasting
  • Solar Irradiance and Renewable Energy Forecasting
  • Photovoltaic Power Modelling and Forecasting
  • Physics-Informed Machine Learning
  • Uncertainty Quantification and Prediction Intervals
  • Spatiotemporal Modelling of Renewable Energy Data

Teaching

Courses taught include subjects related to computer programming, data visualization, machine learning, deep learning and data science.
